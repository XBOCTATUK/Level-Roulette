# <cy>Level Roulette</c>

This mod <cg>adds</c> a <cy>REAL wheel of fortune</c> to <cy>GD</c>.

<cj>Want to know what level you'll get?</c>

## Features
- A <cg>button</c> in the level search screen that opens the popup with <co>Start</c> button
- <cy>A real wheel of fortune</c> that selects a <cj>random level</c> from the list.
- A <co>native tool</c> (can be enabled in the mod settings) for creating lists of levels that you can <cg>switch between</c>.
